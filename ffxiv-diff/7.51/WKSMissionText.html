The traveling spores were likely attached to freight from Sinus Ardorum, and auroral flares have accelerated their growth tenfold. As we have yet to determine what influence this patch of fungi may pose on Auxesia's climate, we reluctantly turn to the most destructive solution: incineration. Thus, we ready our flamethrowers and implore those with the relevant skill sets to contribute to this combustive cause with all due haste.
→389→We have received numerous reports claiming that an overgrowth of ore-rich fruit has sprouted north of the Timberlodge. While this particular species is generally subterranean, auroral flare activity has pushed its growth to the limit, enabling it to sprout towards the surface. Eyewitnesses report that these rarities retract their roots once the flares subside, so we must make the most of this moment if we wish to harvest and further analyze the crop. Those who can provide drill components and other necessary materials during this time will be well rewarded for their efforts.
→390→According to reports, the floracane caused several vacuum suits working in a remote location to malfunction. Though able to return to the southwestern sector of Pileus Pergola, the rapid unscheduled descent destroyed several key components. The suits will cease all operations for the duration of the floracane to prevent further damage, but repairs will be carried out in situ to allow a swift return to action once the danger has passed. We are requesting urgent delivery of materials directly to the crash site!
→391→What a turn of events! The floracane, on a sweep to remove foreign matter from the city, appears to have had a dramatic effect on the Loporrits. Contact with the aetheric petals induces a strangely intoxicated state, and we are receiving reports that a group of Loporrits working in the field has undergone particularly severe exposure. While Loporrits near the base are protected by the rainbow crystal's influence, this leaves our relief efforts short-pawed. Contributors unaffected by the floracane are requested to provide immediate aid to the victims!
→392→One of our drone fleets has suffered a catastrophic system failure due to recent floracane activity. Data readouts indicate the fleet was operating at low altitude when strong winds blew it outside the rainbow crystal's protective ward. While operators were able to safely land the affected units and thus prevent further damage, the incident has necessitated extensive repairs in situ. Contributors are requested to gather the requisite spare parts for the task.
→393→In addition to wreaking havoc on our instruments and beguiling us with their profusion of color, reports show that floracanes induce a frenzy of microbial activity which helps maintain the balance of Auxesia's ecosystem. Pileus Pergola is currently the epicenter of one such microbial bloom, and we wish to collect fungal spore samples to ascertain its impact on soil quality and local flora. We look forward to your valuable contributions.
